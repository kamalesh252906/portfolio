My name is Kamalesh, and I am an aspiring Full Stack Developer from Chennai. My professional path began with a Diploma in Computer Engineering at Central Polytechnic College and is currently being polished at Freshworks STS Software Academy. Coming from a humble background—inspired by my father’s dedication to his flower business and my mother’s support—I have cultivated a strong work ethic and resilience that drives me to stay determined in achieving my professional goals.

In terms of technical expertise, I am proficient in HTML, CSS, JavaScript, PostgreSQL, FastAPI, and Python. My journey includes building responsive static websites and transitioning into complex full-stack web applications. These projects have given me hands-on experience in integrating robust backend logic with seamless user interfaces, ensuring that every digital solution I build is both efficient and scalable.

My time at the academy has been a period of significant growth, where I evolved from being nervous about English communication to receiving a Special Mention for my presentation skills. This personal transformation, combined with my experience in three squad-based projects, has refined my leadership and collaboration abilities. I am now dedicated to becoming a top-tier web developer with deep backend expertise and a commitment to continuous learning.
